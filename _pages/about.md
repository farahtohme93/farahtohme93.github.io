I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Economics at Uppsala University, Sweden.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Goethe University Frankfurt (GSEFM). My research focuses on macroeconomics.

Before joining Uppsala, I was a PhD intern at the International Monetary Fund in 2024 and a Junior Researcher at the Leibniz Institute for Financial Research SAFE from 2020 to 2023.

## Research Interests
- Fiscal and Monetary Policy
- Inflation Expectations and Expectations Formation
- Household Heterogeneity
- Text-as-Data, NLP and Large Language Models in Economics
